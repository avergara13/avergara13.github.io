03 · Decision design

Useful automation has a stopping rule.

RSP automates research and preparation, then stops where judgment matters. The user decides whether to buy, reviews the listing, and explicitly approves publication.

Human decision

A completed analysis can still end in PASS.

The result does not publish or purchase anything. The operator can re-analyze, pass, hold the item as maybe, or move an approved item into the queue.

AI reduces the research and preparation burden. Human judgment remains accountable for the decision and release.

04 · Listing + agent support

The decision becomes reviewable work.

Approved items move into listing preparation. Session context stays available to the in-app agent as supporting evidence, while review and release remain explicit.

I designed the workflow around a real operating decision: capture the item, reduce the research burden, preserve human judgment, prepare the work, and keep the operating context visible.
