02 · Product workflow

From find to listing

The sequence separates observation, assisted work, and human judgment. Each stage produces usable context for the next.

01

Capture

Photograph or scan the item and record the purchase context.

02

Research

AI-assisted identification and comparable-market research.

03

Decide

The user makes the BUY / MAYBE / PASS judgment.

04

Prepare

Approved items move into listing preparation with photos, description, category, pricing, and item details enriched where supported.

05

Review

Required checks and warnings are surfaced before publication.

06

List

Publication is explicitly reviewed and confirmed by the operator.

07

Learn

Sold and outcome evidence updates the operating record where the current implementation supports it.

03 · Decision design

Useful automation has a stopping rule.

RSP automates research and preparation, then stops where judgment matters. The user decides whether to buy, reviews the listing, and explicitly approves publication.

Estimated resale value$64–$82
Acquisition cost$18
Fee + shipping allowance$24
Expected contribution$22–$40

AI reduces the research and preparation burden. Human judgment remains accountable for the decision and release.

04 · Learning loop

The workflow gets smarter from outcomes.

RSP carries the decision past research and listing preparation. Outcome evidence can return to the operating record so future evaluations have better context where the current implementation supports it.

I designed the workflow around a real operating decision: capture the item, reduce the research burden, preserve human judgment, prepare the work, and carry the outcome back into the system.
